Anatomy

1. KINETIC HEADING

Big "PORTOFOLIO" outline + horizontal marquee text di belakang. Marquee bergerak terus subtly.

2. LIQUID GLASS CARDS

4 cards (1 per proyek), masing-masing gradient brand color, backdrop-blur, inner highlight, drop shadow glow.

3. SPOTLIGHT SYSTEM

1 card di tengah jadi "spotlight" — lebih besar, glow lebih kuat, ada label ★ + CTA pill. Rotasi otomatis tiap 4-5s.

4. PROGRESS INDICATOR

4 dashes di bawah, yang aktif lebih lebar + warna accent. Klik dashes untuk jump ke card.

Interaction

  • Entry: Cards stagger fly-in dari bawah dengan blur→focus (450ms each, 80ms apart), magnetic snap saat landing
  • Auto spotlight: Setiap 4.5s, card aktif berpindah ke kanan (smooth scale + glow shift, background gradient juga bergeser)
  • Hover (desktop): Magnetic lift — card mengangkat & sedikit miring mengikuti cursor, glow membesar. Card lain redup 0.6 opacity.
  • Click: Buka Dribbble di tab baru (sama seperti sekarang)
  • Touch (mobile): Swipe horizontal antar cards, dot indicators klikable
  • Idle interaction: Subtle mouse-parallax pada cards (3-5px tilt) + glow yang breathing

Visual Treatment

  • Glass surface: backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(140%), gradient brand 65%→25% opacity, inset white 1px highlight, border 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.15)
  • Spotlight glow: Triple-layer shadow — drop 40px, brand 60px, accent ring 90px (lebih lembut, breathing)
  • Card content: Project image di dalam dengan rounded inner mask, overlay gradient gelap dari bawah, label number "01" dengan letter-spacing besar
  • Typography: Project name pakai Outfit Black, oversized di spotlight (26-32px). Caption Inter 10-12px uppercase tracked.
  • Background: Radial gradient (warna spotlight) berubah perlahan dengan crossfade saat spotlight bergeser
  • Marquee text: "SELECTED WORK · 04 PROJECTS · ONE JOURNEY · SCROLL TO EXPLORE" outline text, scrolling continuous, opacity 0.35

Architecture (file changes)

  • index.html — replace .portfolio-section markup (intro/outro/cards/caption → new structure)
  • js/portfolio.js — rewrite. Drop scroll-pin, drop circle→arc morph. Build hybrid spotlight engine.
  • style.css — replace .portfolio-* block (~220 lines) with new liquid glass styles
  • Data: reduce 8 cards → 4 cards (1 image per proyek, pakai shot terbaik per project)
  • Assets: existing PNG di data/work/ dipakai ulang (Cronicle, Wellpad, Raya, Maxim)
